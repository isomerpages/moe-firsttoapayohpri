<h3><strong>Primary 1 (P1) registration</strong></h3>
<p>Primary 1 registration is a process to register your child for mainstream
primary schools in the following year. The exercise is conducted online
by the Ministry of Education (MOE) from June to August each year.
<br>
<br>Registration is carried out in different phases, based on your child’s
eligibility. Parents should register their child in the phase they qualify
for.</p>

<h4><strong>How to register</strong></h4>
<p>Parents of Singapore Citizen (SC) and Permanent Resident (PR) children
can register through the P1 Registration Portal using their Singpass.&nbsp;
<br>
<br>On the portal, you can:</p>
<ul data-tight="true" class="tight">
<li>
<p>View your child’s eligible registration phases and schools</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Register your child during their eligible phases, from Phase 1 to 2C Supplementary.</p>
</li>
<li>
<p>Check registration results on results announcement days. (You will also
be notified of the results through SMS.)</p>
</li>
</ul>
